electric boilers for heating

Flexiheat UK's electric central heating boilers cover outputs from 0.5kW through to 45kW. This allows an electric boiler system to be matched to a wide range of domestic applications. Low-output electric boilers may be appropriate for smaller heating zones, apartments, highly insulated homes or extensions. Larger electric boilers can provide the heating capacity required by properties with greater heat losses, subject to the available electrical supply.

This straightforward operating principle makes electric central heating boilers particularly relevant to UK properties where access to mains gas is unavailable or unsuitable. More than two million UK homes currently lack a mains gas supply, while other properties may face practical restrictions that make conventional boiler installations difficult. Listed buildings, flats, apartments, converted properties and homes where an appropriate flue route cannot easily be created are examples where electric boilers for home heating may provide an effective alternative.

Removing the need for a flue can provide considerably more flexibility when selecting an installation position. A traditional boiler must be installed where its flue can comply with applicable requirements, which can restrict where the appliance is positioned. An electric central heating boiler instead needs suitable electrical and water connections together with the installation conditions specified for the particular model. This can make electric boilers useful in properties where internal space or access to an external wall is restricted.

    electric heating boiler

    For some properties, avoiding a flue, oil storage tank or LPG installation can simplify the project considerably. For others, an electrical supply upgrade may represent a significant part of the installation. This is why electric boilers cost different amounts to install from one property to another.

    Electric central heating boilers can also be attractive for properties where the owner wants to retain radiators rather than changing completely to direct electric room heaters. A direct electric boiler heats water for a central wet heating circuit, allowing heat to continue being distributed around the building through water.

    Electric boilers for central heating perform a similar basic function to conventional gas and oil boilers. Water is heated within the boiler and circulated through a wet central heating system to radiators, underfloor heating circuits or other compatible heat emitters. The main difference is how the heat is generated. An electric heating boiler uses electrical heating elements instead of burning a fossil fuel. This means an electric boiler system does not require a gas connection, oil storage tank, LPG cylinders or a combustion flue.

    The best electric boiler for a particular property is therefore not simply the model with the largest heating output or lowest electric boiler price. Correct selection starts with understanding how much heat the building actually requires. Oversizing electric boilers can unnecessarily increase the electrical capacity required, while undersizing a boiler can prevent the heating system from meeting demand during colder weather.

    For small homes and apartments, electric boilers for flats can provide a compact way to operate a wet central heating system without requiring gas. For larger homes, an electric boiler for a large house can provide substantially higher heating output where the electrical infrastructure can support it.

    Cylinder selection should be based on expected household hot water consumption. A property occupied by one or two people normally has different requirements from a large family home containing several bathrooms. Shower use, bath capacity, simultaneous demand and recovery requirements all influence the correct cylinder size.

    Point-of-use efficiency does not mean that electric boilers are automatically the least expensive heating technology to operate. Electric boilers cost money to run according to the quantity of electricity consumed and the tariff charged by the electricity supplier. UK electricity commonly has a higher unit cost than mains gas, making property heat loss, heating schedules and control particularly important when considering electric central heating boiler cost.

    electric boiler system

    Safety characteristics also differ from combustion heating. Because electric central heating boilers do not burn natural gas, LPG or oil, the boiler cannot produce combustion-related carbon monoxide. There is also no gas connection to the boiler and therefore no possibility of a gas leak from the appliance.

    Maintenance can nevertheless be simpler in certain areas because electric boilers do not contain burners and combustion components. There is no burner combustion process to analyse and no conventional combustion flue to maintain. This does not mean an electric boiler system is maintenance-free, but the servicing requirements differ from those of fossil fuel boilers.

    The FHMN3 electric system boiler range includes an outdoor weather sensor and indoor room sensor as standard. Weather compensation allows the heating system to respond to changes in outdoor temperature. When outdoor conditions become milder, the required heating water temperature can potentially be reduced. During colder conditions, the controls can respond to the increased heating requirement.

    Compact dimensions can provide another advantage. Depending on boiler output and model, electric boilers for homes can often be positioned in smaller plant areas than conventional heating equipment. A compact electric heating boiler may be suitable for installation in a utility room, kitchen cupboard or another appropriate internal location. Electric boilers for flats can therefore provide an attractive solution when floor space is limited and external fuel storage is not practical.

    An electric wet central heating boiler still forms part of a pressurised or water-based heating installation, so appropriate installation, commissioning and maintenance remain necessary. Pumps, expansion vessels, valves, controls, cylinders, radiators and system water quality should all be considered as part of the complete central heating system.

    Electric boilers for sale should therefore be compared on more than price alone. Heating output, electrical requirements, modulation, system configuration, control functions, dimensions, hot water compatibility and renewable energy options can all influence whether a particular electric boiler is appropriate.

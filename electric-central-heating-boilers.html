electric boilers for central heating

Electric central heating boilers offer a practical way to provide space heating through a traditional wet heating system without using natural gas, LPG or oil. Flexiheat UK supplies electric central heating boilers for domestic applications with outputs ranging from 0.5kW to 45kW, giving homeowners, installers and property developers a broad selection of electric boilers for different heating requirements. Whether the application involves electric boilers for flats, a modern energy-efficient home, a renovation project or an electric boiler for a large house, the correct boiler should be selected according to the calculated heat loss of the property and the available electrical supply.

Electric central heating boilers UK homeowners install can also be considered alongside renewable electricity generation. Solar PV panels generate electricity that can contribute towards household electrical consumption when generation is available. Selected FHMN3 electric system boilers offer optional solar PV integration, providing additional possibilities when designing a home energy system.

Electric boilers themselves do not create combustion emissions at the property. When renewable electricity is available, the associated carbon impact of operating electric boilers can be reduced further. The environmental performance of an electric heating boiler consequently depends partly on how the electricity used by the property is generated.

Safety characteristics also differ from combustion heating. Because electric central heating boilers do not burn natural gas, LPG or oil, the boiler cannot produce combustion-related carbon monoxide. There is also no gas connection to the boiler and therefore no possibility of a gas leak from the appliance.

Maintenance can nevertheless be simpler in certain areas because electric boilers do not contain burners and combustion components. There is no burner combustion process to analyse and no conventional combustion flue to maintain. This does not mean an electric boiler system is maintenance-free, but the servicing requirements differ from those of fossil fuel boilers.

The FHMN3 electric system boiler range includes an outdoor weather sensor and indoor room sensor as standard. Weather compensation allows the heating system to respond to changes in outdoor temperature. When outdoor conditions become milder, the required heating water temperature can potentially be reduced. During colder conditions, the controls can respond to the increased heating requirement.

    electric boilers for homes

    Flexiheat UK's electric central heating boilers cover outputs from 0.5kW through to 45kW. This allows an electric boiler system to be matched to a wide range of domestic applications. Low-output electric boilers may be appropriate for smaller heating zones, apartments, highly insulated homes or extensions. Larger electric boilers can provide the heating capacity required by properties with greater heat losses, subject to the available electrical supply.

    This straightforward operating principle makes electric central heating boilers particularly relevant to UK properties where access to mains gas is unavailable or unsuitable. More than two million UK homes currently lack a mains gas supply, while other properties may face practical restrictions that make conventional boiler installations difficult. Listed buildings, flats, apartments, converted properties and homes where an appropriate flue route cannot easily be created are examples where electric boilers for home heating may provide an effective alternative.

    Removing the need for a flue can provide considerably more flexibility when selecting an installation position. A traditional boiler must be installed where its flue can comply with applicable requirements, which can restrict where the appliance is positioned. An electric central heating boiler instead needs suitable electrical and water connections together with the installation conditions specified for the particular model. This can make electric boilers useful in properties where internal space or access to an external wall is restricted.

    central heating electric boilers

    Compact dimensions can provide another advantage. Depending on boiler output and model, electric boilers for homes can often be positioned in smaller plant areas than conventional heating equipment. A compact electric heating boiler may be suitable for installation in a utility room, kitchen cupboard or another appropriate internal location. Electric boilers for flats can therefore provide an attractive solution when floor space is limited and external fuel storage is not practical.

    An electric wet central heating boiler still forms part of a pressurised or water-based heating installation, so appropriate installation, commissioning and maintenance remain necessary. Pumps, expansion vessels, valves, controls, cylinders, radiators and system water quality should all be considered as part of the complete central heating system.

    Electric boilers for sale should therefore be compared on more than price alone. Heating output, electrical requirements, modulation, system configuration, control functions, dimensions, hot water compatibility and renewable energy options can all influence whether a particular electric boiler is appropriate.

    electric central heating boiler cost

    Point-of-use efficiency does not mean that electric boilers are automatically the least expensive heating technology to operate. Electric boilers cost money to run according to the quantity of electricity consumed and the tariff charged by the electricity supplier. UK electricity commonly has a higher unit cost than mains gas, making property heat loss, heating schedules and control particularly important when considering electric central heating boiler cost.

    Flexiheat UK's range provides electric central heating boilers UK properties can use for a variety of domestic wet heating applications. With models from 0.5kW to 45kW, electric system boilers, options for open vent installations, electric modulating boilers, weather compensation, app control and optional solar PV integration, there are solutions available for different heating system arrangements.

    For properties without mains gas, homes with difficult flue arrangements, flats with limited installation space and homeowners looking for an electrically powered wet heating system, electric central heating boilers provide an established heating option. Flexiheat UK can supply electric boilers for central heating and hot water applications across a wide range of outputs, helping customers select an electric boiler system that suits the heating requirements and infrastructure of their property.

    electric boilers for flats

    For appropriate open vent systems, Flexiheat UK can supply the FHM3 version without the expansion vessel. A minimum five-metre head, corresponding to approximately 0.5 bar, is required for these electric boilers to operate correctly in the specified arrangement. Understanding whether the property uses a sealed or open vented heating system is therefore an important part of selecting the appropriate electric boiler.

    Electric central heating boilers also eliminate the requirement to store combustible heating fuel at the property. Oil-fired heating requires a suitable oil storage tank, while LPG normally requires cylinders or a bulk storage vessel. Both options introduce additional equipment and require suitable external space. Central heating with an electric boiler requires no on-site fossil fuel storage, which can simplify the overall heating arrangement.

    The ability to adjust outputs between 2kW and 24kW on the FHMN3 range can also help match boiler performance to the actual heating requirement. Correct commissioning remains important because the boiler and controls should work together with the radiators or underfloor heating to provide the required comfort without unnecessary electrical consumption.

    Gas boilers operate differently because combustion and flue losses are involved. Even modern condensing boilers cannot convert every unit of gas energy into useful heating. The actual performance of a gas condensing boiler also depends on system temperatures and whether conditions allow effective condensation.

    The absence of combustion is one of the defining characteristics of electric boilers. Traditional gas and oil boilers burn fuel and therefore require a properly designed flue to discharge combustion gases. Electric central heating boilers produce heat through electrical elements, so there are no combustion products from the boiler. As a result, a conventional combustion flue is unnecessary.

    App control is available for suitable Flexiheat UK electric boilers when an internet module is installed. Remote heating controls allow homeowners to change schedules, monitor energy consumption and control the heating system while away from the property. This can be particularly useful when occupancy patterns change throughout the week.
